# Product Overview

This is a healthcare data analysis project focused on pharmaceutical news and drug shortage tracking. The system collects and processes:

- **ALS/Neurology News**: Structured news data from Navlin News covering pharmaceutical developments, regulatory decisions, and clinical updates in neurology (particularly Alzheimer's disease)
- **Drug Shortage Data**: Comprehensive tracking of pharmaceutical shortages from multiple sources including Drug Shortages Canada

## Key Data Sources

- Navlin News API for pharmaceutical industry news
- Drug Shortages Canada for shortage tracking
- Multi-country coverage with standardized country codes and regional classification

## Primary Use Cases

- Monitoring pharmaceutical regulatory decisions
- Tracking drug availability and shortages
- Analyzing trends in neurology/ALS treatments
- Cross-referencing news events with supply chain impacts
